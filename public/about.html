About

screenplay.design is a working notebook about designing software. It collects case studies, essays, and notes from years of building interfaces, component libraries, and the systems that hold them together.

The case studies walk through real projects from start to finish: the problem, the constraints, the decisions that mattered, and what changed as a result. They focus on the reasoning behind the work as much as the final screens.

The writing covers craft and tooling: design tokens, documentation, accessibility, and how AI is changing the way interfaces get drafted, reviewed, and shipped. Posts are meant to be practical, so you can take an idea and use it the same week.

The site itself is kept deliberately small. Pages are plain HTML, articles are stored as simple structured files, and new stories are published from a lightweight writing page. If something here is useful, or you want to compare notes, get in touch.
